# Mission

The mission of the Computer Science and Engineering department is to:

1. Create a quality academic environment supported by relevant IT infrastructure while empowering faculty and students with emerging technologies.

2. Motivate faculty and students to engage in lifelong learning and foster a culture of research and innovation.

3. Inculcate leadership qualities and entrepreneurial skills among students.

4. Generate opportunities for students to evolve into competent software professionals with a strong sense of social responsibility.

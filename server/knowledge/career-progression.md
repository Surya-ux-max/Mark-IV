# Career Progression

Computer Science and Engineering offers abundant career opportunities across various technology domains.

Graduates are recruited by leading multinational companies and startups. Students also have the opportunity to become entrepreneurs by developing innovative software products or pursue higher education and research.

## Career Opportunities

Graduates can work as:

- Software Developer
- Data Scientist
- System Analyst
- Software Architect
- Network Engineer
- Database Administrator
- Web Developer
- Artificial Intelligence Engineer

The department prepares students with the technical and professional skills required for successful careers in the software industry.

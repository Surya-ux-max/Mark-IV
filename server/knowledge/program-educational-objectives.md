# Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The Computer Science and Engineering program aims to achieve the following educational objectives for its graduates.

## PEO1 – Professional Career

Graduates will build successful careers in computing and information technology domains by applying strong foundations in engineering principles, computer science fundamentals, and professional best practices.

## PEO2 – Higher Education and Lifelong Learning

Graduates will pursue higher education, research, and entrepreneurial endeavors by demonstrating self-directed and lifelong learning capabilities to continuously adapt to emerging technologies, industry trends, and societal needs.

## PEO3 – Innovation and Ethics

Graduates will conceive, design, and implement innovative and ethically responsible computing solutions to address real-world challenges in industry and society.

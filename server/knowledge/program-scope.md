# Program Scope

Computer Science is a dynamic and fast-growing field that has become an integral part of modern life. In today's digital era, almost every industry relies on information technology and software solutions.

Automation has already transformed the way people work and live. As technology continues to evolve, future advancements in automation, artificial intelligence, and computing are expected to create even more exciting opportunities.

The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) program equips students with the knowledge and skills required to thrive in this rapidly changing technological landscape.

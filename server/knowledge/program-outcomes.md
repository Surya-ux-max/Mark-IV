# Program Outcomes (POs)

The Computer Science and Engineering program defines the following graduate outcomes.

## PO1 – Engineering Knowledge

Apply knowledge of mathematics, natural science, computing, engineering fundamentals, and engineering specialization to develop solutions for complex engineering problems.

---

## PO2 – Problem Analysis

Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems to reach substantiated conclusions while considering sustainable development.

---

## PO3 – Design and Development of Solutions

Design creative solutions for complex engineering problems and develop systems, components, or processes that consider public health and safety, whole-life cost, net zero carbon, culture, society, and the environment.

---

## PO4 – Investigation of Complex Problems

Conduct investigations using research-based knowledge, including experimental design, modelling, data analysis, and interpretation to produce valid conclusions.

---

## PO5 – Engineering Tool Usage

Create, select, and apply appropriate engineering techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modelling, while recognizing their limitations.

---

## PO6 – The Engineer and the World

Analyze and evaluate societal, environmental, economic, legal, health, safety, and sustainability aspects while solving engineering problems.

---

## PO7 – Ethics

Apply ethical principles, professional ethics, human values, diversity, and inclusion while adhering to national and international laws.

---

## PO8 – Individual and Teamwork

Function effectively as an individual and as a member or leader in multidisciplinary and diverse teams.

---

## PO9 – Communication

Communicate effectively with the engineering community and society through reports, documentation, presentations, and inclusive communication practices.

---

## PO10 – Project Management and Finance

Apply engineering management principles and economic decision-making in individual and team-based projects within multidisciplinary environments.

---

## PO11 – Life-Long Learning

Recognize the importance of continuous learning, adaptability to emerging technologies, and critical thinking throughout professional life.
